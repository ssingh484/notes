# Ingredients

- Half rotisserie chicken (shredded and finely chopped)
- Half red onion (finely chopped)
- 10 Garlic cloves (finely chopped)
- 5 Green chillies (finely chopped)
- Red pepper flakes
- black pepper
- ginger powder or fresh ginger (very little)
- Chicken Broth 1.5-2 cartons
- Garlic powder
- Cayenne pepper
- MSG
- Half block of chicken boullion cube OR Chicken powder
- Salt (light pinch)
- fresh chopped coriander aka cilantro
- 200g of egg noodles
- 1/4 cup corn starch
# Recipe

- First, saute onions until caramelized
- throw in green chillies, let cook down
- add garlic to saute in the end till smell goes away
- add all spices and bloom for a bit
- add stock and reduce a bit with noodles to cook noodles
- Once noodles are al-dente
- Take out some broth and mix in some corn starch to make a slurry
- Add corn starch slurry back in and mix in
- Add chopped chicken and cook for a bit to get up to temp
- garnish at end with cilantro
