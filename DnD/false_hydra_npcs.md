## NPC Profiles (Short)

- **Liz**: Kind, grandma-esque (in her 60s). Talks sweetly about her husband, wears a cottagecore outfit with a simple texture
- **Mayor Roland Hale (Late 50s):** Gruff exterior, but jumpy. Insists everything is fine – until an answered question about “Mayor’s daughter” causes a stammer (because no one named _Eliza_ has lived in town). Keeps any journals or old letters locked.
    
- **Creel the Hermit:** Thin, nervous old man who never stops humming to block the hydra’s song. Lives in a shack beyond town. He will _actively_ warn the party (“I heard... _them_ out there, trying to sing me to sleep”). He found occult books mentioning a “song eating memory.”
