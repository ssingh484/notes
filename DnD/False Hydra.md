# Town intro

_The road narrows as you descend from the ridge, the packed earth slick with mist. It’s quiet here—unnaturally so. Your footsteps land without echo, swallowed by the heavy air. Ahead, fog curls between the trees, parting just enough to reveal a cluster of rooftops nestled in the valley below. Smoke drifts thinly from a few chimneys, but even that seems uncertain, fading into the grey as if reluctant to linger._

_At the edge of the path, a warped wooden sign leans half-buried in weeds. The letters, flaking and moss-eaten, read: “Welcome to Lacuna Hollow.” The name feels heavy on the tongue, like a word you’ve half-forgotten._

_As you step into town, the quiet deepens. The shutters of nearby cottages are drawn tight. A cart stands abandoned by the well, one wheel missing. A dinner table is visible through a window—set for a meal that was never eaten. The air carries a faint scent of ash and rain, and something beneath that—old stone, old sorrow._

_Every so often, you think you hear a sound: a low, distant humming that might be the wind through the valley… or might not. Then it’s gone, leaving only the silence. You can’t shake the feeling that something important happened here—that the town remembers something you don’t._

# Hooks

- Letter from an old friend - maybe with 2 copies to swap out or with secret ink (reveal only after a wisdom check later into town)
- Start by waking up in the field outside the town - clearly more supplies and an extra bed roll than needed - almost as if there was an extra companion

# Clues
- Liz and Bev - tavern owners - Bev makes great beakfast, locally sourced eggs (at check in during evening). Coffee and toast in the morning, Liz is the sole proprietor, never been married. Feels uneasy and gets angry if grilled.
- Local arrives home as part of the party entering the town. Opens and leaves his door opened behind him. Sign on the side of the door facing outward can be read to say "Please keep closed - beware of Dog". Person says he has never had a dog.
- Church bell rings for the first day and for the second day - no more ringing on the third.
- **Mayor Roland** – Authoritative yet uneasy; deep down he suspects something is wrong with his town but can’t put his finger on it. He brushes off questions with nervous laughter.
- **Old Hermit “Creel”** – A loner living on town’s edge, always wearing earplugs or a scarf. He has scribbled journals full of missing names, and he hisses warnings if the party mentions the hydra.
