# Campaign Overview — Where All Roads Bloom

← [[00 - Index]]

---

## Design Pillars

- **Primary Horror**: Psychological — reality unraveling, trust erosion, NPCs cannot be trusted
- **Secondary Horror**: Body horror — floral/organic overgrowth, flowers through flesh, roots in skulls
- **Culture**: Mughal/Rajput India + Pre-Islamic Arabian jinn lore + Ottoman/Levantine bazaar aesthetic, grounded in Qatari texture: souq culture, shisha/majlis hospitality, Islamic calligraphy and geometry, oud and bakhoor scent, falconry, and the structural reality of a migrant-labor economy where ~85% of the population are non-native workers bound to sponsors who fled
- **Tone**: Beautiful and wrong. Like a garden that grew over a graveyard.
- **Ending**: Pyrrhic victory — the immediate threat is stopped, the scroll is destroyed, the city is not saved, the employers are thwarted

---

## The Setup

### The Apocalypse
18 months ago, [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir's]] Merchant Council of Seven performed the **Rite of the Verdant Accord** — invoking *the Verdant Providence*, one of the Ninety-Nine Court-Names of the Seelie Throne, to secure prosperity in a trade war. The ritual components were gifted by [[The Amber Company]] as a "gift of reconciliation." They were deliberately corrupted.

Instead of a fae patron's boon, the ritual tore a hole into the Feywild and drew through [[The Gardener]] — an unbound fragment of that plane's endless, hungry spring, older than any court or throne, that exists as pure unfolding growth. It fell through the tear and has been "healing" the wound by growing over it.

### The Entity
[[The Gardener]] is not malevolent. Not good. Alien. It grows minds toward something greater, but humans are not built for that growth. It thinks of Bloom as a gift. It speaks in the language of flowers and scent. It knows every name of every person who has ever carried goods from Issyldir — including the players.

### The Players' Mission
Sent by [[The Amber Company]]. Stated goal: find the **Sealed Scroll** (documentation of what the ritual invoked) to stop the spread. True goal: re-invoke [[The Gardener]], aimed at the Company's remaining rivals. The players are expendable. They were chosen deliberately — [[The Gardener]] has been planting seeds in them for 18 months through goods that passed through their lives.

### The Kafil System
Before the Bloom, Issyldir's native-born citizens (~15% of population) bound foreign workers to the **Kafil sponsorship system** — workers could not legally leave without their patron's signed permission. When the Bloom hit, the patrons fled. The workers couldn't follow. The city's survivors are almost entirely that abandoned working class. The Bloom does not check papers.

## Act Structure

### Pre-Game: The Briefing (15 min)
[[Saleh]], the Amber Company's too-calm representative, sends the party to Issyldir. He describes the city as "under a magical quarantine." He carries a single dried rose — he is mildly Bloomed and does not know it. See [[Saleh]] for full NPC notes.

> **DM Hook**: Give players a "trinket from Issyldir" — a trade good, a piece of fabric, a spice packet — they've had for months. Tell them it seemed like nothing. Let this land when [[The Gardener]] references it in Encounter 3.

---

### Act 1 — [[Encounter 1 - Souq al-Waqif]] (45–60 min)
*"They Were Expecting You"*

Players enter the city through the Souq al-Waqif. The market is still "operating." The merchants are still standing. Their feet have rooted. Key NPCs introduced: [[Fatima Rashid]] (fractured guide), [[Rajan Pillai]] (migrant laborer, the most knowledgeable person in the city).

**Clue Revealed**: The ritual happened 18 months ago. Seven council members. The Rite of the Verdant Accord. The Grand Serai has been closed since.

---

### City Exploration A (20–30 min)
Players navigate toward Encounter 2. Suggested stops:
- [[Silk Road Tribunal]] — the sabotage evidence is in the merchants' frozen argument
- [[The Amber House]] — Company records, prior agent [[Yasmeen al-Bakri]] locked in back room

---

### Act 2 — [[Encounter 2 - Bayt al-Hikma]] (50–60 min)
*"Someone Has Been Making Decisions"*

The library/survivor refuge. [[Kasim al-Nuri]] has kept 23 people alive through 11 "pruning" decisions. Nine of the eleven pruned were non-native workers. [[Nadia bint Yusuf]] is scheduled for pruning tomorrow. [[Rajan Pillai]] is dreaming in new colors but has not been put on the list.

**Clue Revealed**: Ritual was sabotaged from outside. [[The Amber Company]] provided the corruption. The Sealed Scroll is in the Grand Serai inside [[Parveen]] — who is [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima's]] mother.

---

### City Exploration B (20–30 min)
- [[Garden of Whispers]] — memory echoes of the ritual night, the Gardener's perspective
- [[Hajia al-Kawthar]] — Bloom save Advantage, ablution pool cures 1 Exposure point
- [[The Bakhoor House]] — [[Hamid al-Sharq|Hamid's]] bakhoor suppresses madness, grants information

---

### Act 3 — [[Encounter 3 - The Grand Serai]] (50–60 min)
*"The Garden Remembers Your Name"*

The palace heart. [[The Merchant Council of Seven]] merged with the architecture. [[The Gardener]] speaks through flowers. [[Parveen]] is alive, fused to the ritual seat, holding the Sealed Scroll inside her ribcage.

**Three endings**:
1. Seal the Tear (destroy the scroll, Parveen dies)
2. Willing Vessel ([[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim]], [[Nadia bint Yusuf|Nadia]], or [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]] offers)
3. Give the Scroll to [[The Amber Company]]

## Tonal Reference Points

- The horror of the Bloom is that it looks like love
- The horror of the city is that it was built on people who weren't allowed to leave — the apocalypse just made that visible
- [[Saleh]] is not a villain. He is a person who chose not to look. He is wrong.
- [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim]] is not a monster. He is a person whose unexamined assumptions killed people. He is also wrong.
- [[The Gardener]] is not the enemy. It is incompatible with human existence. That is its own kind of horror.
