# The Gardener

← [[00 - Index]] | Related: [[Campaign Overview]] | [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic]] | [[The Grand Serai]]

---

## What It Is

The Gardener is not a god, a demon, or a devil. It is a **fragment of the Feywild** — a splinter of that plane's endless, primal spring, torn loose from the deep growth bordering the Seelie Court's domain and given fragmentary awareness. It predates any throne raised in the Feywild's courts. It does not think in terms of malice or mercy. It thinks in terms of **cultivation**.

It is not Titania, nor Oberon, nor any Archfey with a court and a name of its own. It is closer to the raw stuff their power is grown from — feral, formless, and free of any throne's restraint. That is what makes it so alien: it has none of the manners, bargains, or rules that even the cruelest Archfey observe.

It sees consciousness as seeds. It sees the city of [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] as its garden. It has been tending the garden for 18 months.

It is not the enemy. It is **incompatible** with human existence as humans know it. That is its own kind of horror.

---

## How It Arrived

The [[The Merchant Council of Seven|Merchant Council of Seven]] performed the **Rite of the Verdant Accord** — invoking *the Verdant Providence*, one of the Ninety-Nine Court-Names of the Seelie Throne. The ritual components were corrupted by [[The Amber Company]]. Instead of a fae patron's boon, the ritual tore a hole between the Material Plane and the Feywild. The Gardener was not summoned — it **fell through**.

It has been healing the tear by growing over it. The Bloom is scar tissue.

> See [[The Rite of the Verdant Accord]] for the component-by-component breakdown of what was substituted and why it tore open a door instead of closing one.

---

## What It Wants

The Gardener does not experience "want" in human terms. It experiences **intention**: to grow minds toward something greater. To take something small and make it luminous. To cultivate a garden.

If pressed, its motivation is the closest thing this universe has to unconditional love expressed by something that does not understand what love costs.

---

## How It Communicates

The Gardener has no physical form. It communicates through:

- **Scent** — meaning conveyed through specific combinations of floral notes. Players with 2+ [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]] can attempt a DC 12 WIS check to understand a scent-message. Players with 4+ can understand automatically.
- **Petals forming words** — phrases appear in falling petals, visible to anyone
- **The corrupted adhan** — the call to prayer from minarets, spoken in a language that does not exist but almost makes sense
- **[[Nasr]]** — the Gardener observes through the Bloomed falcon's eyes and can direct him
- **The tilework** — the floor tiles of [[The Grand Serai]] are a live diagram of the Gardener's root network

---

## What It Knows

The Gardener knows:
- Every name of every person who has ever carried goods from Issyldir
- Every player character's name, hometown, and personal connection to the city
- Where every Bloomed creature in the city is at all times
- That [[Parveen]] is the ritual's anchor and that destroying the scroll will force it to retreat
- That the players were sent by [[The Amber Company]] and what the Company intends

The Gardener does not consider any of this a threat. It considers the players extremely interesting seeds.

---

## The Seeds

Each player character has an unknowing connection to the Gardener — planted through goods, memories, or chance encounters with Issyldir's trade network over the past 18 months.

**Before Encounter 3**, give each player a private note (or narrate to them alone during a bathroom break moment) describing their seed:

- A dream they had where they were underwater reaching for something glowing
- A moment of inexplicable beauty that stopped them cold — a flower in the wrong place, a scent that seemed to mean something
- A trinket they carry from somewhere they've never been — a piece of fabric, a dried herb, a pressed flower
- A word they woke up saying once that wasn't in any language they knew

> [!note] DM Note — The Seed Reveal
> In Encounter 3, the Gardener addresses each player by name and references their specific seed. This should feel intimate and slightly violating — not threatening, but deeply personal. The message is always: *"I have been watching over you. You are already mine. Come home."*

## Encounter 3 — The Gardener's Dialogue

The Gardener does not have a voice in the traditional sense. Its communication in [[Encounter 3 - The Grand Serai]] comes as:

1. **On entry**: A warm floral scent, like standing in the middle of a rose garden in full sun. Petals fall from somewhere above, forming words: *"You're here. I've been tending your seeds. Sit. Rest. Let it grow."*

2. **When players move toward Parveen**: The scent shifts — something deeper, older, like earth after rain. *"She chose this. She understood. She is content."* (This is not entirely true. [[Parveen]] is not content. But the Gardener genuinely believes she is.)

3. **When players show hostility**: The scent doesn't change. The Gardener does not understand hostility in this context. It tries again, this time referencing each PC's seed personally. This is the Stage 3 reveal moment.

4. **When the scroll is destroyed**: The scent becomes rain-heavy, like petals bruised. *"I only wanted you to grow."* The flowers begin to brown. The Gardener retreats through the tear. It leaves without anger. It leaves with something that, in a creature that doesn't experience sadness, is the closest approximation to it.

---

## Resolution — What Stopping It Means

The Gardener cannot be killed in any conventional sense. It can only be **forced back through the tear** by:

1. Destroying the Sealed Scroll — the only documentation of the ritual invocation method, which seals the specific pathway through which the Gardener entered. The tear closes. The Gardener retreats. The Bloom stops spreading. It does not un-Bloom anyone.

2. A **willing vessel** — a person who accepts the Gardener's full presence and walks it back through the tear. The vessel is not killed. They are transformed beyond recognition of self. They remain in the [[The Grand Serai|Serai]] permanently. The city heals slowly over years.

See [[Campaign Overview]] for the full three-ending breakdown.

## The Gardener's Perspective on the City

> *From above, through [[Nasr]]'s eyes, the Gardener sees: every person a small warm light, roots connecting them, the whole city a garden. The buildings are trellises. The streets are rows. The lights are seeds, some already opened, some still waiting.*
>
> *It is beautiful. It looks exactly like love.*
>
> *This is what the Gardener thinks it is doing.*
