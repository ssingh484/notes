# The Bloom Council

← [[00 - Index]] | Location: [[The Grand Serai]] | Encounter: [[Encounter 3 - The Grand Serai]]
Related: [[The Merchant Council of Seven]], [[The Gardener]]

---

## Overview

The Bloom Council is not a single creature — it is seven individuals merged into a single distributed intelligence through [[The Gardener|the Gardener's]] root network. Each of the seven [[The Merchant Council of Seven|Merchant Council]] members is seated at the council table, fused to their chair, and operates as a **separate targetable node** sharing a collective action economy.

**Victory Condition**: Destroy (reduce to 0 HP) any **3 of 7 nodes**. The Bloom Council loses cohesion and the encounter ends. The remaining nodes do not die — they slump, the flowers around them stilling, the connection to the entity temporarily severed.

## Collective Action Economy

The Bloom Council acts on **Initiative 15**. On its turn, it has a number of actions equal to the number of **active (non-destroyed) nodes**, maximum 7. Each action must be taken by a different active node. The council cannot use the same action type more than **3 times per turn**.

### Node Actions (one per active node per turn)

***Root Lash.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a **DC 14 Wisdom save** or gain 1 [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]] point.

***Bloom Spray.*** One creature within 20 ft. must succeed on a **DC 14 Constitution save** or be **Poisoned** until the end of its next turn and gain 1 Bloom Exposure point. On a success: Poisoned only (no Bloom point).

***Beckoning Whisper.*** One creature within 60 ft. that has **1+ Bloom Exposure** must succeed on a **DC 14 Wisdom save** or use its movement on its next turn to move toward the nearest node (Charmed condition, lasts until start of its next turn).

---

## Legendary Actions

The Bloom Council has **3 legendary actions**, used at the end of another creature's turn. Recharges at the start of the Council's turn.

> ***Tendril Reach (1 action).*** One creature within 30 ft. must succeed on a **DC 14 Dexterity save** or take 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage and be **Restrained** until the end of its next turn.

> ***Bloom Resonance (2 actions).*** All creatures currently in the hall with **any Bloom Exposure** must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or gain **1 Bloom Exposure** point immediately.

> ***One of Us (3 actions).*** One creature with **3+ Bloom Exposure** must succeed on a **DC 15 Charisma save** or become **Charmed** by the Bloom Council, treating all other creatures as hostile, until the end of its next turn. This effect does not work on [[Parveen|Parveen]].

## Tactics

**Early rounds**: The Council prioritizes **Beckoning Whisper** on any PC with Bloom Exposure (drawing them closer) and **Root Lash** on any PC who approaches the council table.

**Mid-fight**: Once any node is destroyed and surviving nodes gain temporary HP, the Council shifts to **Bloom Resonance** (legendary action) to accelerate Bloom Exposure across the party.

**If a PC reaches 3+ Bloom Exposure**: The **One of Us** legendary action becomes the priority — turning a PC against their party for one turn can be catastrophic.

**Fire damage**: The Council's root network is flammable. Any fire damage to a node that rolls maximum damage causes the roots in a 5 ft radius to ignite for 1 round (2d6 fire damage to any creature starting their turn in that zone, ignoring the Council's resistance).
