# Bloomed Guard

← [[00 - Index]] | Encounter: [[Encounter 1 - Souq al-Waqif]] | Related: [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic]]

## Overview

Bloomed Guards are the city's former soldiers and gate keepers, their minds softened into comfortable loops by [[The Gardener|the Gardener's]] influence over 18 months. They are not aggressive by default — they are **welcoming**. They follow their last standing orders (check papers, patrol the market, keep the peace) with the patient unconcern of people who have forgotten why those orders matter.

They become hostile only when their routines are disrupted: if someone tries to leave without papers, if property is damaged, if direct violence is initiated.

**The horror of fighting them**: They do not shout. They do not snarl. They move toward disturbances with the same pleasant, purposeful energy they use for everything else.

> ***Rooted Calm.*** The Bloomed Guard cannot be Frightened or Charmed. It does not react to threats with fear or alarm — only with patient, unhurried action.
>
> ***Bloom Transmission.*** A creature hit by the Guard's Slam attack must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or gain **1 [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]]** point.
>
> ***Welcoming Loop.*** If no hostile action is taken against a Bloomed Guard in a round, it returns to its patrol pattern and does not pursue. It can be "reset" to neutral with a Deception DC 12 ("We were just leaving") or a Persuasion DC 15 ("We have papers — we apologize").
>
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The Bloomed Guard has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the Guard's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
> ___
> ## Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The Bloomed Guard makes two attacks: one with its **Spear** and one with its **Slam**, or two **Spear** attacks.
>
> ***Spear.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage if used with two hands in melee.
>
> ***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or gain 1 [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]] point.
>
> ***Welcoming Grasp (Recharge 5–6).*** The Guard reaches for a creature within 5 ft. The creature must succeed on a **DC 12 Strength saving throw** or be **Grappled** (escape DC 12). While grappled this way, the target must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** at the start of each of its turns or gain 1 Bloom Exposure point.
