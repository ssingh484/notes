> ***Tactical Mind.*** When Kasim fails a saving throw, he can use his reaction to reroll it. He must use the new result.
>
> ***Pruning Instinct.*** Kasim has **Advantage** on attack rolls against creatures that have any [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]].
>
> ***Bloom Compromise.*** At the start of combat, Kasim must succeed on a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw** or spend his first action that round targeting the creature with the highest Bloom Exposure, even if that creature is an ally. If he is that creature, he hesitates (loses his action entirely this round).
>
> ***Authority Presence.*** Creatures of CR 1/2 or lower (including his lieutenants) have Advantage on saving throws while within 30 ft. of Kasim.
> ___
> ## Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** Kasim makes two **Magistrate's Blade** attacks, or makes one attack and uses **Commanding Shout**.
>
> ***Magistrate's Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.
>
> ***Commanding Shout.*** One creature within 30 ft. must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or be **Frightened** of Kasim until the end of its next turn.
>
> ***Expose the Bloom (Recharge 5–6).*** Kasim studies one creature he can see within 60 ft. and announces its Bloom Exposure level to all combatants. Until the start of Kasim's next turn, all attacks against that creature have Advantage.
>
> ## Reactions
>
> ***Counter-Argument.*** When a creature Kasim can see succeeds on a Charisma (Persuasion) check against him during combat, he can impose Disadvantage on that roll (declared before the result is revealed).
>
> ***Interpose.*** When an ally within 5 ft. is attacked, Kasim can impose Disadvantage on the attack roll.

---

## Tactics

**Round 1**: Kasim hangs back, assesses Bloom Exposure levels, uses Expose the Bloom if available. He wants to identify which players are most affected.

**Round 2+**: He closes on the highest-Bloom-Exposure PC, using Pruning Instinct to maximum effect. He uses Commanding Shout to prevent players from flanking him.

**If reduced to 25 HP**: He offers to stand down. *"This doesn't serve either of us."* He means it. If players accept, combat ends. If players refuse, he fights to unconsciousness (not to death).

**He does not kill player characters** unless they demonstrate clear lethal intent first. He is trying to maintain order, not commit murder.

>
> ***Shield Shove.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a **DC 12 Strength saving throw** or be pushed 5 ft. back and knocked Prone.

---

## Positioning

The three lieutenants position to protect the stairwell access and create a line between Kasim and the players. They do not pursue players who retreat to the stairwell — their goal is to hold the floor, not kill.

If Kasim is incapacitated, the lieutenants immediately hold their positions and cease attacking. *"Stand down. Wait."* One will try to stabilize Kasim. The encounter ends.
