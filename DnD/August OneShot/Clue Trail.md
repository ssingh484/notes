# Clue Trail — DM Reference

← [[00 - Index]]

This document tracks every clue in the adventure, where it can be found, what it reveals, and when the three-stage PC reveal occurs.

> For the DM's full answer key on the ritual itself — legitimate components, what the Amber Company substituted, and why it tore a hole instead of just failing — see [[The Rite of the Verdant Accord]].

---

## Three-Stage Player Reveal

The players are not random adventurers — they are personally connected to Issyldir through the Gardener's 18-month reach. This reveal happens in three stages.

| Stage | Where | What Players Learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | [[Encounter 1 - Souq al-Waqif|Souq al-Waqif]] — the fountain inscription | The city's motto references "all who arrive are counted." Subtle: players realize they were expected. |
| **Stage 2** | [[Encounter 2 - Bayt al-Hikma|Bayt al-Hikma]] — The Ledger | Every player's name or hometown appears in the 140-year Kafil ledger. They have been here before, or their goods passed through, or their family's trade route was recorded. |
| **Stage 3** | [[Encounter 3 - The Grand Serai|The Grand Serai]] — The Gardener addresses them by name | The Gardener knows each player's personal Bloom seed — the moment of inexplicable beauty that connects them to the city's root network. The connection was not accidental. |

## Clue Access by Act

### Act 1 (Souq al-Waqif and surroundings)
Players should leave with:
- General understanding that the city is inhabited, not abandoned
- First contact with Fatima, Rajan
- Stage 1 reveal (fountain inscription)
- Initial suspicion about who/what caused the Bloom

### Act 2 (Bayt al-Hikma and optional locations)
Players should leave with:
- The sabotage confirmed or strongly suspected
- The Amber Company's involvement established
- Parveen's existence and location known
- The Kafil system's injustice visible (especially for players who found the ledger and Rajan's file)
- Stage 2 reveal (The Ledger)
- Kasim's moral complexity clear

### Act 3 (The Grand Serai)
Players arrive with:
- Everything above
- Stage 3 reveal (the Gardener's personal knowledge of each player)
- The choice of what to do with the scroll

## Minimum Viable Information

If players skip secondary locations and don't investigate thoroughly, they need these minimum clues to proceed:

1. **The scroll exists and is inside Parveen** — Kasim knows this, will share at Trust 2+
2. **The Amber Company delivered corrupted components** — Kasim inferred this; the manifest is findable at DC 11 in the archive
3. **Parveen is in the Serai** — Kasim confirms; Nadia's journal confirms; the tilework map confirms

Everything else enriches the experience but is not required to reach the ending.
