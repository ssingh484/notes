## Description

**Age**: 53. Heavyset, neatly trimmed grey beard. Fine but understated merchant's clothes — the clothes of a man who can afford ostentation and chooses not to perform it. Permanently smells of sandalwood. There is a faint floral undertone to it that he cannot place and has not noticed.

He carries a single dried rose pinned to his coat. He found it on his desk one morning and thought it was a gift from a colleague. He kept it because it is pretty.

**His left hand shakes very slightly when he discusses [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] directly.** He does not notice this.

---

## Personality

Warm, unhurried. Uses "we" and "together" instinctively. Makes direct eye contact. Listens with genuine attention and responds to exactly what was said. He is good at his job not because he is manipulative, but because he genuinely cares about the people in front of him — at least in the moment.

The horror is that he seems like a good person. Because he mostly is.

---

## Backstory

Third son of a merchant family in the rival city of Rashan — no inheritance, no family backing. Rose through [[The Amber Company]] entirely through the rare ability to make every person in a room feel heard. Closes deals without leaving anyone feeling cheated. Genuinely believes in trade as a civilizing force.

**His complicity**: He did not arrange the ritual sabotage of Issyldir. That was his predecessor, **Harun al-Noor**, who died of a fever three years ago — conveniently. Saleh inherited ledgers showing the substitution of ritual components and chose to read them as ambiguous. He needed the contract. He told himself it was probably nothing. He has not allowed himself to look at those ledgers since.

He has been having beautiful dreams for six months: gardens, warm light, a sense of being known. He attributes this to good health and successful work. He does not know he is already Bloomed.

---

## Knowledge

Saleh knows:
- The city of [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] is under a "magical quarantine"
- The Sealed Scroll will document the ritual and allow the Company to replicate it safely (he believes)
- The players were specifically chosen for this mission (he does not know why — he was told they had appropriate skills)

Saleh does **not** know:
- That the component substitution was deliberate sabotage
- That he himself is Bloomed
- Why the players were really chosen (they are the most Bloomed-adjacent outsiders alive, planted by [[The Gardener]])

---

## The Amber Company's True Agenda

See [[The Amber Company]] for the full faction breakdown. Saleh knows the Company wants the scroll but genuinely believes the goal is controlled application of the entity's power — for good. He is wrong. Some Company leadership has more cynical goals.

---

## Role-Play Notes

**When players ask about the city**: He is warm but vague. "There was a ritual that went wrong. The city is — changed. You'll understand when you see it."

**When players push on the component manifests**: He listens carefully, then says, "Those records are historical. They were my predecessor's doing. I'd like to think we're here to fix it." He means this. The hand shakes.

**If players return with the scroll**: He is genuinely pleased and genuinely relieved. He does not perform relief — he feels it. This makes the betrayal (if players realize his intent) more painful.

**If players accuse him of intentional harm**: He becomes very still. Then: "I made a choice not to look closely at something I should have looked at closely. I am aware of what that means." He will not argue. He will not apologize. He will wait.
