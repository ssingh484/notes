## Description

**Age**: 23. Small, dark-eyed, tends to stand slightly apart from groups — not out of hostility but out of a lifetime habit of positioning herself where she can observe. She always has ink on her fingers. Always. She found more ink at month three of the apocalypse and has been using it ever since.

---

## Personality

Quiet, precise, observational. She phrases things as observations rather than declarations — *"I've been noticing..."* rather than *"I think..."* She looks at the middle distance when formulating thoughts, as if reading something only she can see. She is, beneath the fear, **curious** about what is happening to her. This is either admirable or indicative, depending on who is watching.

---

## Backstory

Her father, Yusuf, was a textile merchant from a northern trading family who relocated to Issyldir when Nadia was six. Her mother died when she was nine. She grew up comfortable and educated but with a large absence in the middle of her childhood. She became a junior calligrapher at the [[Bayt al-Hikma|Bayt al-Hikma]] — copying manuscripts, illuminating documents, learning the formal script for sacred texts. [[Parveen]] had quietly noticed her and was beginning to give her more complex work.

**The cruel irony of her situation**: Yusuf never converted his Kafil status to full Issyldiri citizenship — it was expensive and he assumed it didn't matter. He died three years ago. On [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim's]] records, Nadia is listed as "foreign-origin." She has lived in this city since she was six years old. She considers herself Issyldiri. This consideration has no legal standing.

---

## The Dreams

They started three months after the Bloom. Colors she has no words for. She describes them to players (if they ask, which most won't, and this is meaningful) as: *"Colors that are also feelings. Not metaphorically. The color* is *the feeling. There's one that is—"* She stops. *"There's no word for it. It's the color of being held."*

She has tried to paint them. The pigments are never right. Her room in the library is papered with attempts — dozens of sheets, all slightly wrong, all clearly trying to capture something just beyond what paint can do. She keeps a journal: every dream recorded in the closest words she can find, with failed color illustrations at the margins.

## What the DM Knows

> [!danger] Nadia's True State
> Nadia's Bloom indicators are among the mildest possible. She is nowhere near dangerous. She may never become dangerous.
>
> Her journal contains descriptions of architectural details of the [[The Grand Serai|Grand Serai]] she has never visited — the reflecting pool, the tilework, the shape of the roots in the council chamber. She has been dreaming the city from inside [[The Gardener]]'s perspective.
>
> [[The Gardener]] would describe her as its most successful student. She is learning to perceive without being consumed.
>
> [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]] has been having the same dreams. He is not on [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim's]] list.

## The Journal

Players who find or are given Nadia's journal will encounter:

- 23 entries of colored-dream descriptions, increasingly detailed
- Entry 11: *"I dreamed I was a root. I went from the palace to the library to the fountain to the gate. I could feel everything along the way. Not people — lights. Warm lights with seeds inside them. I think I was looking for something. I think I was looking for me."*
- Entry 19: *"There is a woman in the palace. She is in pain. She has been in pain for a long time. She knows I'm dreaming. She looked at me through the roots. She said something I can't remember."* (This is [[Parveen]] — she has been watching Nadia through the Bloom network)
- Entry 23 (most recent, three days old): *"The new people are coming. I can feel them from here. They carry seeds. Old seeds, from the city. I think the garden has been waiting for them."* (Nadia wrote this before the players arrived)

## Role-Play Notes

**When players first see her**: She is sitting against a wall with her journal, cross-legged, ink on her fingers, not eating. She looks up when players approach without alarm — just attention.

**If players mention the dreams**: She becomes quietly, intensely interested. This is the first time anyone has asked. She will share the journal freely.

**If players tell her she's scheduled for pruning**: Her face goes very still. Then: "Yes. I know." Pause. "I have been waiting for someone to give me a reason." This is not melodrama — she has been genuinely thinking about whether to flee, stay, or accept.

**As a Vessel candidate (Encounter 3)**: If players ask her, she considers for a long moment, then: *"I've been going there in my dreams for two months. Maybe I'm already partway there."* Her sacrifice, if she chooses it, is the most quietly devastating of the three options.
