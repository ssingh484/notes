# Rajan Pillai — Migrant Laborer

## Description

**Age**: 41. Compact, calloused hands, a very short beard. Wears a faded orange blessing cord around his left wrist — tied by his wife the day he left for [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]], 15 years ago. It is fraying. He has never replaced it because replacing it would feel like admitting it has been too long.

He carries a folded piece of paper in his shirt pocket — a sketch of his wife and daughter, drawn from his descriptions by another laborer who could draw. The faces are almost worn away from folding and unfolding. He does not take it out in front of anyone.

---

## Personality

Economical with words. Not cold — genuinely warm when trust is established — but he has learned over 15 years that words cost him more than they cost people with papers. He has a slight eastern accent: rolled Rs, softened Ss.

He uses "friend" as a sincere form of address only when he means it. If he calls you "friend," you have earned something real.

He is patient in a way that comes not from contentment but from having spent 15 years waiting for things to change that didn't.

---

## Backstory

From the coastal province of **Veldur**, far to the east — fishing villages, monsoon seasons, nothing like this. Came to Issyldir at 26 on a six-month labor contract to help build the outer wall extension. The contract renewed. And renewed. He learned the city's layout intimately — every pipe, load-bearing wall, water source, and foundation quirk. He helped build the wall the players just walked through.

He was sponsored under the **Kafil system** by a silk dealer named **Tariq al-Fawzi** — not actively cruel, just indifferent. When the Bloom hit, al-Fawzi fled on the third night and left no exit permission for Rajan. Rajan went to the gate. The guards asked for his exit pass. He stepped back from the gate. That was 18 months ago.

**His daughter**: She was born six months after he left for "just six months." She is 15. He has never met her. He does not mention this unless directly asked. When asked, he answers honestly and then changes the subject.

---

## What He Is Doing Now

- Surviving carefully. Helping other trapped workers quietly — never organizing (that would draw [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim's]] attention).
- Keeping himself away from central districts and known Bloom-dense zones.
- Maintaining the crack in [[Hajia al-Kawthar|Hajia al-Kawthar's]] minaret with mortar every few months. He has not told anyone. No one has asked him to.
- Dreaming in colors he has no words for. He has not told anyone. He has watched what happens to people who mention similar things.

---

## The Pattern He Sees

Rajan has noticed that [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim's]] "pruning" decisions disproportionately affect non-native workers. He has not formalized this observation. He has simply kept his head down and not mentioned the dreams. 

> [!tip] If Players Have High Trust With Rajan
> He will point out the pruning pattern without being asked — quietly, as an observation, not an accusation. "I have been counting. Of eleven. Nine were workers." He will not elaborate unless asked. If asked: "I have been dreaming in new colors. I have not told him."

---

## Knowledge

Rajan knows:
- The full structural layout of [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] — every building, pipe, water source, back alley
- Which water sources are still clean
- Which buildings are structurally sound vs. compromised by the Bloom
- The location of every significant survivor group in the city
- How the Kafil exit system worked (he's intimately familiar with its mechanics)
- The company manifest for the ritual components — he was part of a warehouse crew that unloaded those deliveries. He remembers the unusual smell of one crate.

---

## Role-Play Notes

**When first met** (Encounter 1): He watches the players from across the market. Does not approach. If players ignore him or talk to him as a resource to extract, he gives minimal information. If players treat him as a person — ask his name, ask how he's doing, make eye contact — he warms noticeably.

**Persuasion DC 12** (or simply treating him with basic human dignity): He opens up and offers to guide the party. He knows every back alley. He also mentions, flatly, that Kasim "decides who stays and who doesn't" in the Bayt al-Hikma. His tone makes clear this is not a compliment.

**At Encounter 3**: If players have cultivated trust with Rajan, he follows them to the [[The Grand Serai|Serai]]. When [[Parveen|Parveen]] explains the willing vessel option, Rajan quietly offers himself — without drama, without martyrdom. *"I have nowhere else to go anyway."* This is either quietly heroic or quietly devastating depending on how the players have treated him.
