## Description

A **saker falcon** — a large, powerful species, now larger than he was. His wing-feathers are iridescent at the edges, flowers growing from where the primary feathers meet the secondaries: small, five-petaled, luminous white. A single blue flower grows from the place on his head where a falconry hood would normally rest.

He does not blink at a normal rate. His eyes are amber and unnervingly still.

He follows the players throughout the city from the first time they see him in [[Souq al-Tayr|Souq al-Tayr]], always at a distance, always watching. He lands on high points — cornices, minaret ledges, the tops of walls — and observes with the patient attention of a creature that has seen everything that has happened in this city for 18 months.

---

## Personality

Nasr is not a simple creature. He has been Bloomed into something slightly more than a falcon — not human, not divine, but in between. He has opinions about what he has seen. He has watched [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim's]] pruning decisions from above. He watched [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]] patch the minaret crack in the rain. He has been watching [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima's]] circuits around the [[The Grand Serai|Serai]] for 18 months.

He cooperates with [[The Gardener]] because the arrangement suits him — perpetual flight and the knowledge of everything below. He is not controlled. He accepted.

---

## History

**Personal falcon of [[The Merchant Council of Seven|Tariq al-Shams]]**, Council Head. Kept as a status symbol — trained by Issyldir's finest falconer, fed the best meat. Tariq brought Nasr hooded to the Rite of the Verdant Accord as a symbol of his mastery. In the first moment of the Bloom, Tariq's hand opened involuntarily. Nasr flew. He was in the air when the pulse hit — caught partially, enough to be touched, not enough to be lost.

## Mechanics

### Vision Share
A creature with **2+ [[Bloom Exposure Mechanic|Bloom Exposure]]** within 60 ft. of Nasr can, as a bonus action, attempt a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw**. On a success, they receive a flash of Nasr's accumulated memories — 18 months of aerial surveillance of [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]], conveyed as up to 1 minute of overlapping visions. The player can ask for:

- **A map-level layout of the city** — all key buildings, current Bloom density zones
- **A memory of a specific person** — Nasr has watched everyone in this city at some point
- **The night of the ritual** — seen from above; key visual: Tariq al-Shams's expression is **surprise**, not fear — as if something did not go according to a plan he expected to go according to plan

### DM Guidance Tool
If players are missing a critical clue, Nasr lands near the relevant location and watches it. **DC 12 Animal Handling** to recognize this as intentional guidance rather than coincidence. On a failure, players feel a slight uncanny sensation — a falcon watching a door too intently — but don't consciously register it. On a success: *"The falcon is not just perching. It is looking at something specific."*

## Nasr's Opinions (DM Notes)

These are never directly communicated but can be hinted through the Vision Share:

- He watched [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim]] make his pruning decisions. He does not judge by human moral frameworks. But he noticed the pattern.
- He has followed [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]] through the city many times. He considers Rajan worth watching — there is something in Rajan's careful, quiet work that interests him.
- He is aware that [[Parveen]] is in pain. He has been sending her images of the sky. He is not sure she can receive them. He sends them anyway.
