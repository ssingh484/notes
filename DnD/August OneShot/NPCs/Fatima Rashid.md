## Description

**Age**: 28. Lean, quick, dark-braided hair kept short. She wears her father's old messenger satchel — still over her shoulder out of pure habit, 18 months after the world ended. A flower has grown from the satchel's brass clasp. She has not mentioned this to anyone.

She taps her fingers against the satchel strap in a repeating pattern whenever she is frightened — the old Issyldir messenger tap code, the one runners used to confirm receipt. She does this unconsciously. Players who notice and succeed on a DC 13 History or Insight check can identify it as a code.

---

## The Two Memories

Fatima has two fully realized, mutually contradictory sets of memories. She switches between them without warning, apparently triggered by stress, certain locations, or mention of her mother.

**"Present" Memory**: 18 months have passed. The Bloom is real. Her mother [[Parveen]] is somewhere inside the [[The Grand Serai|Grand Serai]]. She has been surviving in the outer city. She is terrified and exhausted.

**"Before" Memory**: The ritual happened last week. Her mother is at the [[Bayt al-Hikma|library]] right now. The city is fine. She just needs to deliver this message. Everything is going to be fine.

> [!tip] DM Tell
> **When she is in the "Before" memory**, she calls her mother *"Ami"* (mother). Her voice lightens. She gives directions confidently — to places that no longer exist in the state she describes.
>
> **When she is in the "Present" memory**, she calls her mother *"the Scholar."* Her eyes don't quite focus. She gives accurate directions but may stop mid-sentence and look at her hands.
>
> Track which mode she's in. Let players learn the tell and act on it.

---

## Personality

In "Present" memory: Rapid, precise, functional. She is doing what she does — running a route, completing a job. She has compartmentalized almost everything.

In "Before" memory: Calm, purposeful, almost serene. She talks about her mother's research with the warmth of someone whose relationship is intact. This is the most painful mode to witness.

---

## Backstory

Her father was a message-runner; her mother, [[Parveen]], was Issyldir's Head Scholar. Fatima grew up between two worlds — too educated for the working quarter, too working-class for the educated class. She resolved this by being useful to everyone, belonging to no one. She became the fastest message-runner in the city and served informally as [[Parveen|Parveen's]] courier between the library and the Serai. She knew every street, shortcut, and guard rotation.

**The night of the ritual**: She was delivering [[Parveen|Parveen's]] sealed letter of objection to the Grand Serai as the doors closed. She was too late. She heard the ritual from outside — heard the note held too long, like a string about to break. She ran. She knew which gaps to use. She survived.

She has been circling the [[The Grand Serai|Grand Serai]] for 18 months without being able to go inside. She does not fully understand why she cannot make herself go in. The "Before" memory makes it easier not to think about it.

---

## Knowledge

Fatima knows:
- The general layout of Issyldir from before the Bloom (streets, shortcuts, building purposes)
- That the ritual happened at the Grand Serai and involved the Council
- Who [[Kasim al-Nuri|Kasim]] is and where the [[Bayt al-Hikma|Bayt al-Hikma]] is
- Where her mother works (was/is — both sets of memory agree on this)

Fatima does **not** know (in either memory set):
- That [[Parveen|her mother]] is merged to the ritual seat inside the Serai
- The specific details of the Rite of the Bountiful Name
- That the players were deliberately chosen by [[The Gardener]]

## Role-Play Notes

**When players ask her about the Serai**: She stops. Long pause. Then either: "I don't go there" (Present), or "The Council is meeting in chambers, I'll wait outside" (Before). Do not elaborate unless pushed.

**When players mention her mother**: This is the most reliable trigger for switching memories. The DM can use this deliberately — push Fatima into Present memory when players need accurate information, and into Before memory when you want to create doubt.

**When players ask about the messenger tap code**: She looks at her fingers, surprised. She teaches it to them if they ask kindly. It's the most herself she looks in the entire adventure.
