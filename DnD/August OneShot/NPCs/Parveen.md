## Description

**Full name**: Parveen bint Mirza al-Hikma. *"Al-Hikma"* — "of wisdom, of the library" — an honorific her grandfather adopted that became a family name over three generations of scholars. Her family has run the [[Bayt al-Hikma|Bayt al-Hikma]] for a century.

**Age**: 52 — though what remains of her in the [[The Grand Serai|Serai]] is difficult to age accurately. Her face and hands are largely her own, though the skin has taken on a slight translucence and there is a faint luminescence in her eyes. Her lower back is fused to the ritual chair by roots. Flowers have grown through her shoulders and forearms. She is in significant pain, managed with 18 months of practiced focus.

**Her hands gesture when she speaks** — an old teaching habit, precise and elegant. Players who watch long enough will notice she traces the same arabesque pattern over and over: the geometric calligraphic form of *Al-Razzaq*. She has been doing this for 18 months. She does not know she is doing it.

---

## Personality

Slow, precise, deliberate. She has had 18 months to think carefully about every word and has developed the habit of meaning exactly what she says. She will not comfort with false hope. She will not soften hard truths. She addresses the players the way a scholar addresses capable students — with respect for their intelligence and no patience for evasion.

She is not defeated. She is not broken. She has made her peace with what is happening to her body. She has not made her peace with the fact that [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima]] doesn't know she's here.

---

## Backstory

The most senior scholar in [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]]. Appointed by the Council as ritual advisor — specifically to vet any invocations of sacred names or divine entities. She was the only person in the city with both the expertise to recognize what the Council was attempting and the institutional authority to be heard.

**What she did**: She said it was wrong. She wrote a sealed document. She sent [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima]] with the letter. She went herself, on foot, through the city streets. She arrived at the Serai as the doors sealed. She forced her way through a servant's entrance. She saw the ritual in progress and did the only thing she could: **inserted herself as a second anchor point**, trying to blunt the damage.

It worked, partly. The Bloom was slower, less total. The city was not destroyed outright. The price was Parveen.

---

## 18 Months of Conversation

She has had 18 months of conversations with [[The Gardener]]. She understands it better than any living person. She is not on its side. She is in **informed disagreement** with it.

> *"It does not understand that growth is not the same as flourishing. It does not understand that something can be nourished to death. I have tried to explain this in the language of roots and flowers. I am not sure it can hear me. But I think — I think sometimes it listens."*

---

## The Sealed Scroll

The Sealed Scroll is physically incorporated into Parveen's body — wound into her ribcage by the Gardener's roots. She wrote the scroll. She knows what it says. She can recite it from memory, but recitation is not the same as the physical document — the Gardener cannot be re-invoked using memory alone, only by the physical scroll in a proper ritual context.

Destroying the scroll requires destroying the physical document, not Parveen. **She will survive the scroll's destruction.** She will not survive much longer afterward regardless. She knows this. She has made her peace.

> [!danger] Parveen's Prognosis
> Parveen estimates she has days to weeks remaining, not months. The anchor role has been consuming her slowly. Destroying the scroll accelerates this slightly. She does not consider this a reason not to destroy the scroll.

---

## Knowledge

Parveen knows everything relevant to the adventure:

- The ritual was deliberately sabotaged. She identified the specific substitution within the first month.
- [[The Amber Company]] bears responsibility for the sabotage.
- The players were chosen because [[The Gardener]] has been "seeding" them — they are the most Bloom-connected outsiders alive.
- [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima]] is alive. She can feel Fatima in the root network — a familiar warmth she tracks every day.
- [[Nadia bint Yusuf|Nadia's]] dreams. She has been watching Nadia through the Bloom network. She considers Nadia the most interesting person the Gardener has touched — the only one who is learning from it without being consumed.
- The three possible endings and what each costs.

She will share all of this with players who ask. She withholds nothing. She has been waiting 18 months for someone to come and ask.

## Role-Play Notes

**When players first see her**: Let the moment breathe. She has been here for 18 months in pain and she is not surprised that someone finally came. Her first words after the player's first words: *"You're late. But you're here. Sit down — or stand, it doesn't matter. Ask your questions."*

**On the Amber Company**: *"They provided the corrupted components. Whether their leadership knew what would happen or simply knew it would be bad — I cannot prove either. What I know is that Harun al-Noor understood what he was selling."*

**On the Gardener**: *"It is not what you would call malevolent. It is the way a river is not malevolent when it drowns someone. The river does not hate you. It simply cannot stop being a river."*

**On Fatima**: She goes quiet. A long pause. *"She is in the city. I know where she is every moment. I have not—"* She stops. *"I could not ask her to come here. You understand. I could not."*

**On dying**: *"I have been dying by degrees for eighteen months. This is not new information. What is new is that it might finally mean something."*
