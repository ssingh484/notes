## How Players Find It

The Hajia al-Kawthar announces itself by sound — the correct adhan, at the correct time, sounding like a human voice. Every other minaret in [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] has been calling at wrong hours in wrong voices. Players who pay attention to the adhan while navigating the city can follow the correct call to this location. No check required — the contrast with the corrupted calls is unmistakable.

## History

The oldest building in Issyldir — predates the city itself by approximately 50 years. A small prayer house existed at the original trading crossing, used by passing merchants long before anyone thought to build walls and call it a city. The city grew around it. It has been expanded four times by different donors with different aesthetics: the oldest section (the prayer hall itself) is spare, simple, and powerful; the newest section (the outer courtyard added 40 years ago) is ornate to the point of excess. The discontinuity between sections is architectural history made visible.

**The spring**: The building was constructed at this location because of a deep spring — reliable clean water at the desert crossing. The ablution pool is fed by this spring. The spring is deeper than the building's foundations, and deeper than [[The Gardener|the Gardener's]] roots have penetrated. This is why the water is still clean.

**The qibla error**: The qibla wall (the wall indicating the direction of prayer toward Mecca) is technically three degrees off — an error made when the city was too young to have precise astronomical data. Every imam since has known this. It has been considered the building's endearing imperfection for 90 years.

**The Bloom's correction**: The flowers that have grown on the qibla wall have arranged themselves in a geometric pattern that exactly corrects for the three degrees. The Gardener did not intend this — it grew along the lines already present in the tile geometry. The correction is mathematically precise. **DC 18 Arcana or Religion** to recognize what this means: [[The Gardener]] grew the flowers along the exact path that would have been "correct" if the original builder had known the exact coordinates. The Bloom completed something humans left unfinished.

## Entering the Mosque

> **READ ALOUD**: *"You hear the mosque before you reach it — the correct adhan, sounding from the minaret on what your instincts tell you is actually the right time. Every other minaret in Issyldir has been calling at wrong hours, in wrong voices. This one sounds like a person. You follow it.*
>
> *The Hajia al-Kawthar has been decorated by the Bloom the way a gift is wrapped — carefully, as if with respect. The flowers on the far wall have arranged themselves into an arch that mirrors the mihrab exactly. The main hall is mostly clear: only a few tendrils of root at the baseboards, some petals on the floor. The air here is different — the floral sweetness that saturates the rest of the city is present, but fainter. Underlaid with something older. Stone and water and time.*
>
> *The ablution pool is full. The water in it is the clearest water you have seen since you entered Issyldir. Your hands, lowered into it, look like your own hands. Just yours."*

## The Minaret

The minaret has a crack running from its base, caused by the original Bloom pulse 18 months ago. The crack has been patched — carefully, with fresh mortar — multiple times. Players who examine the minaret base closely (DC 11 Investigation) see the patches clearly: different-aged mortar fills, methodically applied.

**If players ask who patched it**: Nobody knows. Most survivors have never noticed. [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima]] (if present, Present memory) says: *"Someone's been keeping it standing. I always thought it might fall. It hasn't."*

**If players ask [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]]**: He looks at the minaret, then at the players. A long pause. *"Yes. I've been keeping it up. Someone had to."* He does not elaborate unless pushed. If pushed: *"The city is quiet enough already without losing the correct call."*

## What Players Can Do Here

- Rest safely (short or long rest — DM may allow one long rest for plot pacing if players need it)
- Remove Bloom Exposure points via the ablution pool (1 per visit, 10 minutes)
- Examine the qibla flowers for the three-degree correction (revelation about the Gardener's nature)
- Use the correct adhan timing to navigate — the mosque is a landmark visible from the upper city
- Have a private moment away from Bloom influence (important for players at 3+ Exposure who are struggling with madness)
