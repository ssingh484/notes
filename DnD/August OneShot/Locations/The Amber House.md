## History

The Amber Company has maintained a commercial presence in Issyldir for 90 years — a trade office, records space, and hospitality suite for Company agents passing through. The Amber House is their facility: a mid-sized building in the merchant quarter, tasteful and utilitarian, showing the confidence of people who expect to be doing business here indefinitely.

**Harun al-Noor** was the Company factor who arranged the component substitution. He left Issyldir three months before the ritual — the substituted components had already been delivered. His departure was logged as routine rotation. His correspondence, left in the archive files because he believed no one would read them, documents his deliberate work. See [[The Rite of the Verdant Accord]] for exactly what he substituted and how he knew where to look.

**Yasmeen al-Bakri** was the junior Company agent left as liaison and building manager. She was here when the Bloom occurred. She has been here since. She has been in the back room for three months, breathing shallowly, not moving much, the Company's note still on the door.

## Description

> **READ ALOUD**: *"The Amber House is one of the few buildings in the upper merchant quarter that doesn't look like it has been abandoned — the sign is still polished, the windows (clouded with Bloom growth on the exterior) are still intact. The front door is closed. There is a note on it, written in a precise administrative hand: 'CLOSED FOR INVENTORY. Return business inquiries to Company offices in [city name, two weeks' travel away]. Authorized personnel only.' The note has been there long enough for the sun to bleach the ink at the edges."*

> **READ ALOUD (inside)**: *"The interior of the Amber House is what a trading company's field office looks like when it has stopped being used but not been abandoned — papers on desks, ink dried in wells, a chair pushed back as if its occupant just stood up. The Bloom has grown slowly here; there are flowers at the baseboards and a rose growing from the window box outside the glass, but the interior is navigable. The archive room is clearly labeled. The door to the back room is closed. From behind it, you can hear — very faintly, barely above breathing — a sound that takes you a moment to identify. Someone in there is humming. The same few notes, over and over, without variation."*

## The Archive Room

The Amber Company is organized. Their archive room is impeccably labeled and cross-referenced.

| File | DC | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Component delivery manifest | 10 | The "gift of reconciliation" shipment. Weight, count, packing method — the stylus, ink, and glyph primer are itemized alongside the two genuine components. See [[The Rite of the Verdant Accord]] for what each item actually was. |
| Substitution notes (Harun's) | 13 | Harun al-Noor's records of what was replaced and with what. Written in margin shorthand. **DC 14 Arcana** to understand the significance. |
| Correspondence — Harun to Company HQ | 15 | *"Components delivered. Ritual scheduled in 3 months. Departure booked. The city should be ready for reassessment by year-end."* Written 22 months ago. |

| **The prize**: Harun's full report | 15 | Filed under routine reports, unlabeled. Documents the expected outcome of the ritual corruption — city-wide Bloom, permanent, Company to offer "restoration services" for a fee. **The smoking gun.** |

## Yasmeen al-Bakri

Behind the back room door: Company junior agent, 28, three months into a state that is something between hibernation and surrender. She has been humming the same fragment of a song since the first month. She is not Bloom-touched in the usual way — she is simply very still, very calm, and very done with pretending.

The note on her door: *"BACK SHORTLY. Do not disturb."* It was a joke she made to herself in the first week. She has not left since.

> **READ ALOUD (opening the back room door)**: *"She is sitting in a high-backed chair with her hands in her lap, looking at the wall. She turns when you open the door, without surprise, as if she had heard you coming from the archive room and decided to wait. She is young — younger than her composed expression suggests. On the floor around her chair: three months of careful marks on the wall, one per day, scratched with a stylus. She stopped making them thirty-one days ago. She says: 'Hello. I've been meaning to unlock that door.'"*

**What Yasmeen knows**: Everything Harun al-Noor did. She was his assistant. She prepared the manifest. She didn't know until the ritual failed what the substitution was intended to produce — she thought it was sabotage of a rival's operation. She didn't know people would die. She has known, for 18 months, what she is responsible for.

**What she will do**: Cooperate fully with players, tell them everything, provide the archive evidence, and ask — with genuine uncertainty, not manipulation — what happens to her now. She is not a villain. She is someone who was complicit and never faced it until there was nowhere to go.
