## History and Description

The Souq al-Tayr — Bird Market — was a specialty section of the greater Souq, famous for the saker falcons used by the city's merchant families for ceremonial hunting and prestige display. The falcon trade was a marker of status in Issyldir: every Merchant Council family maintained at least one bird, and Tariq al-Shams's personal bird, [[Nasr|Nasr]], was the most prized in the city.

When the Bloom occurred, every bird in the souq left. The cages are open and empty. [[Nasr]] stayed — he is Bloomed and connected to the Gardener's network, and the city is where the connection is strongest.

> **READ ALOUD**: *"The Bird Market is quiet in a way that's distinct from the rest of the city's silence — quieter, somehow. The cages are still there: brass, lacquered wood, hanging from frames at multiple heights, all open, all empty. The perches inside are marked by feathers that have been there long enough to go brittle. At the top of the tallest cage frame — a large open platform designed for falcons — a single saker falcon sits, watching you. It has flowers growing from the feathers of its wings: small, precise, growing in the exact pattern of its plumage. It tilts its head. It is waiting to see what you will do."*

## [[Nasr]] Contact

**DC 12 Animal Handling** to approach without startling him. On success: Nasr allows approach. He will share a vision if a player comes within arm's reach and makes a specific calming gesture (DC 10 Animal Handling).

See [[Nasr]] NPC notes for full vision share mechanics.

**If players fail the Animal Handling check**: Nasr startles, rises to a height of 40 ft, circles twice, and returns. They can try again after 10 minutes.

**What the vision shows**: Aerial view of Issyldir from before the Bloom — the city in commerce, crowded and working. Then the same view 18 months ago: the pulse from the Serai, moving outward in a ring of flowers. Then Tariq al-Shams's face — Council Head, at the moment the pulse hit him. His expression: surprise, not fear.
