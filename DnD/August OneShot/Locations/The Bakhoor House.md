## History

The Bakhoor House has been in the same family for three generations. [[Hamid al-Sharq|Hamid's]] grandfather established it as a working bakhoor workshop — burning and blending incense for ritual and commercial use. The surname "al-Sharq" — "of the East" — was an identifier his grandfather acquired because he came from somewhere east of Issyldir and the city couldn't remember exactly where. The name stayed. It became the family's.

The building's current function as a Bloom-repellent space was an accident. The concentrated bakhoor smoke — burned in large quantities from pre-Bloom stock — has kept the Bloom from penetrating the walls. [[Hamid al-Sharq|Hamid]] noticed within three months of the Bloom event that his building was clear and other buildings were not. He has maintained the burn continuously since, at considerable personal supply cost.

He has not told anyone this could be replicated. He is waiting to be asked directly.

## Description

Inside, the air is layered with smoke — not acrid, but rich and deliberate, the kind of smell that feels like it belongs in a place that has always been used for thought and conversation. Small charcoal burners sit in the corners, the bakhoor logs glowing amber.*

## [[Hamid al-Sharq|Hamid's]] Locked Box

On the counter behind Hamid, a locked box. The keyhole is blocked — a small flower has grown into it from the wood of the box itself. The flower has been there for three months.

If players ask about it: *"It contains something I've been keeping until someone came who might know what to do with it. I'll open it when I know what kind of people you are."*

The box requires **Trust level 3** with Hamid or **DC 16 Persuasion** to access. Inside: a full vial of concentrated bakhoor oil — enough to create a Bloom-free zone of 30 ft radius for 8 hours. Effectively a portable version of this building. Mechanically: one use, extremely useful in the final encounter if players choose combat.

## Social Notes

The Bakhoor House is where Issyldir's merchants used to conduct private conversations — deals too sensitive for the public souq, arguments that needed quiet witnesses, relationships conducted away from family observation. It is a house with the social understanding that what is said here is not repeated.

[[Hamid al-Sharq|Hamid]] maintains this tradition. He will not volunteer information about other survivors, other locations, or anything he's observed — but he will confirm information players already have, and he will answer direct questions honestly. He is the most straightforwardly informative NPC in the adventure. You just have to ask exactly the right questions.

**What he knows that players won't think to ask**: The Amber Company agents who delivered the components 19 months ago were unusually well-dressed for merchants. Their hands were scholar-soft. They smelled of something other than the road. He didn't think much of it at the time.
