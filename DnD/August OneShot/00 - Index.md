# Where All Roads Bloom
### D&D 5e One-Shot | Levels 4–6 | 4–6 Players | ~4–5 Hours

> *A merchant city swallowed by a ritual that invited something ancient and alien — something that thinks it is being kind.*

**Setting**: [[Issyldir - The City]] — a walled merchant crossroads city, post-apocalyptic, floral body horror, psychological dread. Inspired by Qatari and Mughal cultural aesthetics.

## Three Endings

1. **Seal the Tear** — Destroy the scroll, Gardener retreats, city static, [[Parveen]] dies. Pyrrhic.
2. **Willing Vessel** — [[Kasim al-Nuri]], [[Nadia bint Yusuf]], or [[Rajan Pillai]] volunteers. City heals slowly. They cannot leave the Serai.
3. **Give the Scroll to the Company** — [[The Amber Company]] wins. Seeds for a campaign.
