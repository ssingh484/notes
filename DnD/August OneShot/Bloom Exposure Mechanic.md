# Bloom Exposure Mechanic

← [[00 - Index]] | Related: [[The Gardener]] | [[Campaign Overview]]

---

## Overview

The **Bloom Exposure Track** measures how deeply [[The Gardener]]'s influence has penetrated a character's mind and body. It is tracked per character from 0 to 6. At 6, the character is **Fully Bloomed** — absorbed into the Gardener's garden, alive but no longer themselves.

The Bloom is not purely negative. At low levels, it grants a strange kind of perception. This is intentional — the Gardener thinks it is improving the characters.

---

## The Track

| Points | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Clean | No effect |
| 1 | Seeded | Flowers begin appearing in peripheral vision. Purely aesthetic, but unsettling. |
| 2 | Sprouting | Roll on **DMG Short-Term Madness table**. Lasts until long rest. |
| 3 | Rooting | **Disadvantage on all Wisdom saving throws** until cured. |
| 4 | Tended | [[The Gardener]] knows the character's **exact location at all times**. The character may feel watched. |
| 5 | Flowering | Roll on **DMG Long-Term Madness table**. |
| 6 | Full Bloom | Character is taken by the Gardener. They remain alive but become an NPC. They have no memory of their previous life. **They are happy.** |

> [!warning] Full Bloom
> A character reaching 6 Bloom Exposure is not dead — they are transformed. Their player may describe a moment of genuine peace on their face before the change completes. This should feel tragic, not punishing.

## Gaining Exposure

| Trigger | Save | Failure Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Accepting food/drink from Bloomed merchants | DC 12 CON | +1 Bloom |
| Touching a Bloomed creature directly | DC 11 CON | +1 Bloom |
| Being hit by a Bloomed Guard's Welcoming Grasp | DC 12 CON | +1 Bloom |
| Entering the Garden of Whispers ([[Garden of Whispers]]) | DC 15 WIS | +1 Bloom |
| Staying in the Grand Serai for 1+ minutes without engaging | DC 13 WIS | +1 Bloom |
| Looking into the Serai's Reflecting Pool (2+ Bloom) | DC 13 WIS | +1 Bloom |
| Destroyed Bloom Council node pulse ([[Bloom Council]]) | DC 15 CON | +1 Bloom |
| Using Bloom Resonance Legendary Action target (2+ Bloom) | DC 15 WIS | +1 Bloom |

> [!tip] Advantage at the Mosque
> All Bloom Exposure saving throws made inside [[Hajia al-Kawthar]] are made with **Advantage** due to the sacred geometric architecture's natural resistance.

## Cures

| Method | Effect | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Ablution Pool ([[Hajia al-Kawthar]]) | Remove **1 point** of Bloom Exposure | Hajia al-Kawthar |
| Parveen's Remote Influence | Remove **1 point** per hour of rest | [[Bayt al-Hikma]] only |
| Bakhoor House rest (30 min) | Removes 1 **Short-Term Madness** + Advantage on next save | [[The Bakhoor House]] |

> [!note] Parveen's Cure
> [[Parveen]] can reduce a character's Bloom remotely through the flower network — but only while the character is resting in the Bayt al-Hikma. A piece of her mind reaches everywhere through the roots. She knows who is in the library at all times.

## Incidental Benefits (DM Discretion)

The Bloom is not purely a threat. The Gardener genuinely believes it is improving the characters. DMs may offer the following as flickers of Bloom-enhanced perception:

| Points | Optional Benefit |
|---|---|
| 1+ | Advantage on Perception checks in areas with heavy Bloom growth |
| 2+ | May attempt DC 13 WIS check to receive images from [[Nasr]]'s perspective |
| 3+ | Can hear the correct word beneath the corrupted adhan (see [[Encounter 3 - The Grand Serai]]) |
| 4+ | The tilework in [[The Grand Serai]] becomes readable — the character understands the Bloom map without a check |

## DMG Madness Tables (Reference)

### Short-Term Madness (d10, lasts until long rest or cured)

| d10 | Effect |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | For 1 minute, the character is overwhelmed with the urge to eat. The food they seek is flowers. |
| 2 | For 1 hour, the character hears a name being called — their own name, in a voice they almost recognize. |
| 3 | For 1 hour, the character treats the nearest Bloomed NPC as a trusted friend. |
| 4 | For 1 minute, the character is paralyzed by the beauty of a flower, plant, or vine within sight. |
| 5 | For 1 hour, the character becomes convinced one ally has flowers growing under their skin. |
| 6 | For 1 minute, the character's vision is tinted rose-gold and everything smells of jasmine. |
| 7 | For 1 hour, the character feels the city breathing around them and must succeed DC 12 WIS to take aggressive actions. |
| 8 | For 1 hour, the character is compelled to touch every carved or written instance of *the Verdant Providence's* sigil they see. |
| 9 | For 1 minute, the character forgets the current year and speaks as if it is 18 months ago. |
| 10 | For 1 hour, the character experiences profound serenity. They have Disadvantage on Initiative rolls. |

### Long-Term Madness (d10, requires *greater restoration* or narrative cure)

| d10 | Effect |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | The character develops a compulsion to collect and press flowers. They become anxious if separated from their collection. |
| 2 | The character no longer sleeps — instead they stand motionless for 6 hours at night, eyes open. They receive the benefit of rest. |
| 3 | The character speaks in a second voice simultaneously — a quieter, calmer voice that sometimes says different things. |
| 4 | The character grows a single flower somewhere on their body. It is painless. It will not stop growing. |
| 5 | The character develops amnesia about how they arrived in Issyldir. Each morning they believe it is their first day here. |
| 6 | The character becomes convinced that one person they have met is not real — that the Gardener is wearing them. |
| 7 | The character can no longer lie. They do not know they cannot lie. |
| 8 | The character can smell emotions. Fear smells like smoke. Love smells like jasmine. Greed smells like copper. |
| 9 | The character no longer feels pain. They also no longer feel the warning pain of injury — they must track HP carefully. |
| 10 | The character hears the Gardener's voice at all times. It is never threatening. It is never silent. |
