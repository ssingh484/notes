## The Setup

Players have just entered [[Issyldir - The City|Issyldir]] through the main gate. The briefing from [[Saleh]] told them to find a contact named Fatima Rashid in the market. What he did not tell them: the city is not simply "quarantined." The city is still running — which is somehow worse.

## Opening — The Gate (Zone A)

> **READ ALOUD**: *"The gates of Issyldir are open. Two guards stand at the passage — armored, upright, facing forward with the patient attention of soldiers who have been on post for a very long time. Too long. As you approach, one of them turns toward you and smiles — a slow, warm smile, unhurried, the smile of someone who has been waiting for a specific person and has finally seen them arrive. 'Papers,' he says. His voice is pleasant. His feet, where the armor ends and the cobblestones begin, have disappeared into the stone. Roses have grown up around his ankles. He does not appear to notice. 'Papers,' he says again, still smiling."*

**If players provide papers** (or any kind of identification): The guard reviews them — or mimes reviewing them, holding whatever is presented without actually reading it — and waves them through. *"Welcome to Issyldir. Your stall has been kept warm."*

**If players ask about the feet**: The guard looks down, then back up. *"Standard post. Move along."*

**If players are aggressive or try to push past**: The guard (and his partner) shift from welcoming to restraining in the same unhurried way. Use the [[Bloomed Guard]] stat block if combat is triggered.

## Navigating the Souq

### Tone Notes
- Merchants track movement with their heads but do not break from their positions
- The shisha pipes in Zone G curl smoke upward through flowers growing from the coals
- The oud and bakhoor from Zone D fuses with the Bloom's floral sweetness into something headache-sweet
- The adhan sounds from a nearby minaret — three hours early, in a voice that is almost human. DC 15 Perception: beneath it, the original melody, like a wire being bent

### Zone E — Central Fountain
The fountain is dry. Flowers fill the basin. The marble inscription on the base is partially obscured by roots but legible with a DC 11 Investigation: *"Given to the people of Issyldir, in whose labor we flourish."* The irony of this, given the city's Kafil history, is available to any player who thinks about it.

### Zone J — Sana's Essences (Horror Beat)
> **READ ALOUD**: *"The flower stall at the end of the deep market has the largest, most vivid flowers you have seen since entering the city — enormous, iridescent, petals shifting color in the light. The stall sign reads 'Sana's Essences — Fine Perfumes and Attars.' Behind the counter, a woman tends the flowers with the gentle attention of a craftsperson at work. She does not look up as you approach. One of the flowers, you notice, is not a flower. It is a hand — fingers spread, petals growing from the knuckles — the wrist disappearing into the earth of the stall floor. 'My best work,' the woman says, still not looking up. 'My husband grew that one.' She is entirely calm. She has been standing here for eighteen months."*

No saving throw is required for this encounter. It is purely horror flavor.

## Key NPC Encounters

### Finding Fatima Rashid
[[Fatima Rashid]] is found in Zone B or C depending on her current memory state. She is either: moving purposefully between stalls checking for a message she needs to deliver (Before memory) or standing very still against a stall wall watching the guards (Present memory).

**How to determine memory state**: Roll a d6 at the start. On 1–3, she is in Present memory. On 4–6, Before. Track this — it may shift during the encounter.

**Present memory opening**: *"You're from outside. You actually came. I've been telling people — I've been—"* She stops, takes a breath. *"My name is Fatima. My mother is — the Scholar is at the library. She can help you. I can get you there. Don't accept anything they offer you to eat."*

**Before memory opening**: *"Ah, good, you must be the Amber Company delegation. I have a message for you from the library — there's been a concern raised about the ritual timing, but I'm sure the Council—"* She trails off. Something crosses her face. *"I'm sorry. I'll have that delivered."*

### Meeting Rajan Pillai
[[Rajan Pillai]] is in Zone C or H, not with the guards, not with the rooted merchants. He watches from a position of practiced distance. He will not approach first.

**Opportunity to engage**: Any player who looks around carefully (DC 12 Perception) notices that one person in the market is not acting like the others — he's watching, not standing, not repeating. A player who makes eye contact with him can attempt to approach.

**If players approach Rajan** with basic human decency (introduce themselves, ask his name): He answers. Warily at first, then with increasing openness if the players demonstrate they're treating him as a person and not a resource. No check required for this — it is a social RP moment, not a dice roll.

**If players ask Rajan for guidance**: Persuasion DC 12 (with Disadvantage if players were rude or dismissive) or automatically succeeds if players treated him well. He offers to guide them. He also says, flatly: *"The man who runs the group at the library is named Kasim. He decides who stays and who doesn't. That is not a compliment."*

- [x] Seven council members were involved
- [x] It was called "the Rite of the Verdant Accord"
- [x] The [[The Grand Serai|Grand Serai]] has been closed since then

## If Combat Triggers

**Trigger**: Players attack a guard, attempt to forcibly drag a rooted merchant free, or destroy stalls/property in a way that disrupts the market's routine.

**Initial response**: The guards at Zone A converge. 2 [[Bloomed Guard|Bloomed Guards]] from the entry, 1 more from Zone F arrives in Round 2. They do not shout, warn, or threaten — they simply move toward the disruption with the same pleasant, purposeful energy they do everything else.

**Escalation**: Each round of sustained combat, one additional Bloomed Guard emerges from the deep market (Zone I). Maximum 8 guards total. They do not pursue outside the market's boundaries.

**The goal**: Surviving (not winning) — players can disengage and flee toward the Bayt al-Hikma at any time. The guards do not follow beyond the market.

> [!tip] Running This Encounter
> Resist the urge to make the Souq feel like a dungeon. It is a market that happens to be a horror. Players should spend most of their time talking to [[Fatima Rashid|Fatima]] and [[Rajan Pillai|Rajan]], looking at things, and feeling deeply uncomfortable. Combat is a failure state here, not a goal state.
