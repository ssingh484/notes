# Trust Tracker — NPC Relationships

← [[00 - Index]]

Track each NPC's trust level with the players (0–5). Each level unlocks different information, cooperation, or roleplay outcomes.

---

## How Trust Works

Trust advances through:
- Treating the NPC with respect and genuine interest
- Providing them accurate information (not always complimentary)
- Following through on stated intentions
- Acting in ways consistent with the NPC's values

Trust decreases through:
- Deception discovered
- Violence against people the NPC cares about
- Dismissing their concerns or reducing them to a function

Trust does not reset to 0 from a single interaction unless the players do something genuinely devastating (kill someone the NPC loves, etc.). It is granular, not binary.

## [[Fatima Rashid]] — Messenger's Daughter

| Level | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 0 | Won't initiate conversation; responds to direct questions in monosyllables |
| 1 | Will engage in basic conversation; gives information freely available in the city |
| 2 | Tells players about Parveen (her mother) — "She went into the Serai. I couldn't stop her." |
| 3 | Will guide players through the city from memory; shares the tap code (Persuasion DC reduced by 4) |
| 4 | Her "Before" and "Present" memory states synchronize — she becomes more stable |
| **5** | **She tells players that when she touches roses now, she can hear her mother's heartbeat** — the Gardener's connection. She asks to come to the Serai. |

**Starting Trust**: 1 (she wants to be helped; she just doesn't know if players are trustworthy yet)

## [[Kasim al-Nuri]] — Magistrate-Survivor

| Level | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 0 | Formal interview mode; guarded; will not share the notebook |
| 1 | Confirms that Parveen is in the Serai and the scroll exists inside her |
| 2 | Shows the notebook (23 survivors, 11 crossed out); admits the pattern is "a thing I've been thinking about" |
| 3 | Discusses his predecessor Harun and the sabotage evidence; lets players access archives freely |
| 4 | Acknowledges the pattern in the pruning directly — "I've been trying not to see it." |
| **5** | **Offers himself as the willing vessel** — quietly, without drama. "It seems like appropriate penance." |

**Starting Trust**: 1 (he wants to assess them, not befriend them; he is open to trust but needs reasons)

**Trust breaks**: If players reveal they work for the Amber Company without additional context, Trust drops to 0 immediately. Repairing this requires the full sabotage evidence and a DC 16 Persuasion check.

| Level | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 0 | Polite, withdrawn; will not answer personal questions |

| 2 | Shows players his sketch of his wife and daughter (faces worn away); asks nothing in return |

| 4 | Points out the racial pattern in Kasim's notebook (no check required at this Trust) |
| **5** | **Offers himself as the willing vessel** — three beats of silence, then: "I have nowhere else to go anyway. And this city built me." |

**Rajan's Trust advance**: Rajan's Trust advances fastest if players treat him without condescension — ask his name before his occupation, don't assume his story, let him finish sentences.
